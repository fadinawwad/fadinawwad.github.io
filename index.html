About

I’m Fadi, a Creative Director based in Toronto with 17+ years across advertising and digital design. I’ve led work for multinational brands at agencies including Bounteous, Horizon FCB, Leo Burnett and Saatchi & Saatchi, across Toronto, Dubai and Amman.

Today my focus is UI/UX: websites, mobile and app experiences that are as considered as the brands behind them. I lead with the idea, build design systems that scale, and use AI-assisted workflows to move faster without losing craft.
